IO-LEVEL-R07-NATIVE-PEAK-QUEUE-RETENTION

R07: native meter peak retention across rejected DSP reports. The physical R05 tests established intermittent missed metronome peaks upstream of Home drawing. In the original SHARC meter primitive, the accumulated peak is unconditionally cleared even when the stock event queue rejects its publication. R07 retains the measured peak until the next successful normal publication. The display is the exact working R05 M4 binary. R06 and its defective segmented renderer are excluded.

Seven existing SHARC instruction slots are replaced in place; no instruction-count increase, no added bootstream records, no changed metronome kernel. The candidate changes one SHARC payload sector and its CRC header. The stock success path and sample acquisition are tested using the actual original machine instructions. It remains unproven that queue rejection caused the physical missed metronome clicks.
